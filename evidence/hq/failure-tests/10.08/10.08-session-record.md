## Failure

```text

interface Port-channel30

```

Routed interfaces were left up.

```text

  Po20 administratively down/down
  Po30 administratively down/down
  Vlan160 10.10.60.3 up/up

hq-d1:
  Vlan160 10.10.60.2 up/up
  HSRP VLAN160 Active local / peer unknown

hq-a1:
  Po10 Root/FWD
  Po20 down
```

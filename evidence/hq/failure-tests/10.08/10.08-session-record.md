## Failure

Port-Channel shutdowns, HSRP isolation and surviving OSPF neighbours.

<details>
<summary>Show original evidence and measurements</summary>

```text

```

Routed interfaces were left up.

Immediate failed state:

```text

hq-d1:
  Vlan160 10.10.60.2 up/up
  HSRP VLAN160 Active local / peer unknown

hq-a1:
  Po10 Root/FWD
  Po20 down
```

</details>
